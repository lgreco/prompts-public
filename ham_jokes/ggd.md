# Greek, German, Dutch

## Task

Write original jokes about three friends: a Greek, a German, and a Dutchman. Base each joke on the character traits and shared context described below, and favor punchlines that play on the contrast between the three personalities.

## Setting

All three are licensed ham radio operators holding the highest-rank license (US Amateur Extra). They live in the US, in or near Chicago, and meet regularly for ham radio club events. They all speak and write excellent English with noticeable continental accents.

## Characters

* **The German**
  * Brilliant engineer
  * Dry sense of humor
  * Very direct
  * Generous with his time
  * When in Germany, he drove French cars

* **The Dutchman**
  * Skilled IT executive
  * Passionate about cars with an excessive number of cylinders (anything more than 4 is excessive)
  * Speaks French

* **The Greek**
  * College professor
  * Speaks French
  * Flies a small airplane
  * Fond of German cars and the Dutch Caribbean

## Output

Produce two sets of jokes, each in its own file, in the same directory as this file. Do not include commentary or explanations outside the jokes themselves in either file.

* **`ggd-jokes.md`** — Write each joke as a short, self-contained scene or exchange — setup and punchline — not a generic riddle format. Number the jokes in the order generated.
* **`ggd1-jokes.md`** — Write each joke in the classic "walked into a bar" form: "A Greek, a Dutchman, and a German walk into `<venue>`" (choose a fitting venue per joke — it doesn't need to be a bar). Permutate the order of the three nationalities across jokes so no single ordering dominates. Number the jokes in the order generated.
